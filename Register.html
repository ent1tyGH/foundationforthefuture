Register your Business

Check in with these local offices in the Philippines to make sure your business is properly registered.

Department of Trade and Industry

Handles business name registration and supports the growth of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) through various development programs and initiatives.

Securities and Exchange Commission Philippines

Regulates corporations, partnerships, and associations, and oversees the securities market to ensure fair and lawful operations.

Bureau of Internal Revenue

Makes sure businesses follow tax laws and requirements. Registering here is necessary to operate legally, stay tax compliant, and prevent penalties.

Food and Drug Administration Philippines

Ensures the safety, effectiveness, and quality of products. Provides an official list of licensed establishments and registered health products, giving consumers access to safe and reliable goods in the market.
