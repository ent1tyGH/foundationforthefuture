Get Certified

Take your business to the next level and earn recognition as a truly sustainable brand. Visit these agencies to obtain accreditation that validates your commitment to sustainability.

BRCGS (Brand Reputation through Compliance Global Standards)

They certify product safety, quality, and regulatory compliance in cosmetics and personal care.

COSMOS (Cosmetic Organic and Natural Standard)

They ensure the products meet environmental and health standards from raw materials to manufacturing, packaging, and labeling.

GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice)

This is a system that ensures that manufactured products are consistently produced and controlled according to set quality standards.

Green Choice Philippines

This is an ecolabelling program aimed at promoting environmentally preferable products and services in the Philippines.

B CORP

It certifies overall business performance in sustainability, ethics, governance, social, and environmental impact.

Fairtrade International

Certifies ethical sourcing, fair wages, and equitable trade conditions for producers in developing countries.

PETA’s Beauty Without Bunnies

Ensures and certifies products are cruelty-free, with optional vegan certification; no animal testing in any production stage.

NATRUE

They certify natural and organic cosmetic products using strict ingredient criteria.

Leaping Bunny

Certifies products and ingredients are not tested on animals at any stage of development.
